## PROMPT BASE

Character model sheet for the mascot of EL BRAVO, a burger restaurant. 2D illustration only.

REFERENCE IMAGES: Image 1 is the current El Bravo logo: use it only for the character's identity (the mask and face); ignore its old composition, lettering, background and the star. Images 2 to 15 are other brands' identities, used only for the qualities listed below: Images 2-5 Korean Tiger, Images 6-8 Guksu Café, Images 9-11 I Scream, Images 12-15 Ornico. Never copy their characters, animals, names or logos.

CHARACTER: a Mexican MMA fighter wearing a lucha libre mask. Keep the mask design from Image 1 exactly: red mask with darker red seams; a gold trim around each eye that sweeps up and outward into pointed tips; thick black lining inside the eye holes; the two eye trims meet in a V above the nose, with the red forehead coming down into the V; red nose area; a gold frame around the open mouth and chin; tan skin showing at the eyes, mouth and chin. Remove the star from the forehead completely and leave the forehead plain red: no star, no flag, no emblem, no political or ideological symbol anywhere. Do not add new details to the mask. Colors: mask red #D7261E, gold #F2AE2E, skin #C8743E, outline #1A0D08. Body: stocky MMA build, black fight shorts, open-finger MMA gloves. Personality: the calm, centered strength of a fighter, brave in the ring and friendly at the counter, always grinning.

WHAT TO TAKE FROM EACH BRAND:
- Guksu Café (Images 6-8): the strength of a centered, composed fighter, like a martial artist: grounded stance, calm confidence. The way the character is drawn: few colors, flat color, no shading, outline of the same thickness everywhere. A character sheet with numbered poses and faces.
- Korean Tiger (Images 2-5): the mascot interacting with the food, even hugging a real dish; the head alone works as a sticker and as an icon; its bold, graphic illustration style.
- Ornico (Images 12-15): its sticker poses can become El Bravo poses: burger worn as a hat, lying under the bun, hugging an ingredient, eating at the table, running with a drink. The head can peek in from the bottom edge.
- I Scream (Images 9-11): the crazy, playful attitude: exaggerated expressions and comic-book energy (halftone, rays, bursts).

LAYOUT: one presentation sheet on a clean off-white background.
- Left, large: full-body hero pose in a centered fighter stance, putting a giant burger in an arm-lock, grinning.
- Top right: the head alone, three-quarter view facing right, smiling, with a white die-cut sticker border.
- Middle right: three head expressions, same three-quarter view facing right: big grin, confident wink, roaring laugh.
- Bottom right: two small full-body poses with food, inspired by the Ornico stickers: wearing a burger bun as a hat; lying under a giant bun.

STYLE: [STYLE]

Constraints: 2D only, no 3D render, no photorealism, no text, letters or logos anywhere, no star on the forehead, the same character and mask in every panel, nothing that looks like the reference animals.
