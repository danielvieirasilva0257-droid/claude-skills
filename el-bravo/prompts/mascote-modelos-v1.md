## PROMPT BASE

Character model sheet for the mascot of EL BRAVO, a burger restaurant. 2D illustration only.

CHARACTER (Image 1 is the identity reference, keep it): a Mexican MMA fighter wearing a lucha libre mask. Keep the mask design from Image 1 exactly: red mask with darker red seams; a gold trim around each eye that sweeps up and outward into pointed tips; thick black lining inside the eye holes; the two eye trims meet in a V above the nose, with the red forehead coming down into the V; red nose area; a gold frame around the open mouth and chin; tan skin showing at the eyes, mouth and chin. Remove the star from the forehead completely and leave the forehead plain red: no star, no flag, no emblem, no political or ideological symbol anywhere. Do not add new details to the mask. Colors: mask red #D7261E, gold #F2AE2E, skin #C8743E, outline #1A0D08. Body: stocky MMA build, black fight shorts, open-finger MMA gloves. Personality: brave in the ring, friendly at the counter, always grinning.

WHAT TO TAKE FROM EACH REFERENCE (use only these qualities; never copy their characters, animals or brand names):
- Image 2 (Guksu Café): the way the character is drawn: few colors, flat color, no shading, outline of the same thickness everywhere; a character sheet with numbered poses and faces.
- Image 3 (Korean Tiger): the mascot interacting with the food, even hugging a real dish; the head alone works as a sticker and as an icon.
- Image 4 (Ornico): the mascot playing with the food: burger worn as a hat, lying under the bun, hugging an ingredient; the head can peek in from the bottom edge.
- Image 5 (I Scream): the crazy, playful attitude: exaggerated expressions and comic-book energy (halftone, rays, bursts).

LAYOUT: one presentation sheet on a clean off-white background.
- Left, large: full-body hero pose, putting a giant burger in an arm-lock, grinning.
- Top right: the head alone, three-quarter view facing right, smiling, with a white die-cut sticker border.
- Middle right: three head expressions, same three-quarter view facing right: big grin, confident wink, roaring laugh.

## BLOCOS DE ESTILO

V1, tom sobre tom (Guksu): Flat vector with at most four colors (red, gold, skin, white). Every outline is drawn in a darker red #8E1611 instead of black, same thickness everywhere; the eye lining stays the darkest tone. Simple rounded shapes, big head on a compact body (head about half the body height). Calm, clean, premium.

V2, adesivo de contorno grosso (Korean Tiger + Ornico): Bold cartoon sticker style: thick uniform dark outline #1A0D08 around every shape, flat colors, no gradients, chunky chibi proportions, and every figure with a white die-cut border like a premium vinyl sticker sheet. Friendly and punchy.

V3, doido feito à mão (I Scream): Loose hand-inked cartoon: wobbly brush line with natural thickness variation, flat color fills slightly off the line, rubbery exaggerated anatomy, huge mouth and bulging eyes in the expressions, small halftone dots, speed lines and comic bursts behind the poses. Messy on purpose, full of energy.

V4, pôster de lucha libre em risografia: Mexican lucha libre poster printed in risograph: only three inks (red, golden yellow, dark brown), visible grain, halftone shading, slight misregistration between the inks, bold heroic poses with dramatic foreshortening, sunburst rays behind the hero pose.

V5, HQ e anime: Comic book and anime influence: confident ink line with varied weight (thicker on the outer silhouette), one step of hard cel shading in a darker tone of each color, dynamic action poses, speed lines, sharp expressive eyes. Still 2D and clean, like a modern comic character sheet.
